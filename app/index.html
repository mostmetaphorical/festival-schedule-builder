Take it with you

Without that box ticked, the saved page holds only your schedule and is safe to share or host publicly. Tick it and the file also contains your rating history — useful as a backup, but it is personal, so don't post it anywhere public.

Stores your ratings and plan in this browser only, so you don't have to import them again. Nothing is sent anywhere and there's no account. Browsers do clear this sometimes — the downloaded file is the reliable backup.

Help make the recommendations better

This is an alpha. The model behind it was trained on an old public dataset of mostly mainstream films, and it's being tested against real film-festival slates — where most titles are premieres nobody has rated yet. More real rating histories make it measurably better, and there's an honest test in the repository that scores how well it predicts ratings it has never seen.

If you'd like to help, send your ratings.csv to festrecommender.crucial122@passmail.net.

What to send, and what not to
  1. On a computer, sign in and open letterboxd.com/settings/data.
  2. Click Export your data. A .zip downloads.
  3. Open the .zip and take out ratings.csv only.
  4. Send that one file. Please don't send the whole .zip.

ratings.csv holds only the date, film title, film year, a link to the film, and your star rating. Your username, real name, email, location, bio, reviews, diary and watchlist are in other files in that zip, which is exactly why only this one is wanted. If you'd rather not share when you watched things, delete the Date column first — it isn't used. It's only useful if you've rated 15 or more films.

Ratings stay on your device. Film details come from TMDB — this product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.
